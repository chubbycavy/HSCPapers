What HSCPapers is

HSCPapers is an independent, study-first index of NSW Higher School Certificate material: 7,000+ trial papers, past HSC exams and school assessment tasks, organised Subject → Year → School and split into HSC (NESA past papers) and Trial (school-written).

It hosts no PDFs itself — every download resolves to its source: the community mirrors HSC Portal and PapersDB, the Board of Studies archive, and official NESA links. Catalogue metadata mirrors THSCOnline and rebuilds automatically every night.

🐢 Slow-route papers exist only behind THSC’s rate-limited resolver; they’re hidden by default — flip the sidebar toggle when you want everything visible.

How to get the most out of it

· Share any view — search and filters live in the URL (e.g. ?q=ruse&type=trial)

· 📖 Read opens any paper in-page; the ⏱ timer keeps you honest

· Select several papers for a structured ZIP — folders arrive as Subject/Year/, solutions included

· 🐢 Slow-route papers hide by default; flip the toggle when you want everything visible

· For whole-library downloads, install the desktop app — queued, resumable, ~30 MB/s, straight into your own folders. ⭳ Get the HSCPapers desktop app
