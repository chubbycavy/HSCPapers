What HSCPapers is

HSCPapers is an independent, study-first index of NSW Higher School Certificate material: 7,000+ trial papers, past HSC exams and school assessment tasks, organised Subject → Year → School and split into HSC (NESA past papers) and Trial (school-written).

Most downloads resolve to their source: the community mirror HSC Portal, the Board of Studies archive, and official NESA links — plus a small self-hosted set of papers from our own library. Catalogue metadata mirrors THSCOnline and rebuilds automatically every night.

🐢 Slow-route papers exist only behind THSC’s rate-limited resolver; they’re hidden by default — flip the sidebar toggle when you want everything visible.

📄 Found a paper we’re missing? Submit it — verified submissions go live self-hosted, same day.

How to get the most out of it

· Share any view — search and filters live in the URL (e.g. ?q=ruse&type=trial)

· 📖 Read opens any paper in-page (paper only — ＋ Solutions shows solutions side by side, 🔗 turns pages together); the ⏱ timer keeps you honest

· Select several papers for a structured ZIP — folders arrive as Subject/Year/, solutions included

· 🔗 Share a selection — one link restores the exact same papers on anyone’s screen, ready to ZIP

· 🐢 Slow-route papers hide by default; flip the toggle when you want everything visible

· 📥 Installable — add the site to your home screen; the catalogue and recently read papers work offline

· For whole-library downloads, install the desktop app — queued, resumable, ~30 MB/s, straight into your own folders. ⭳ Get the HSCPapers desktop app

Content & removal

All papers remain the property of their schools/authors and NESA — this site is a free, non-commercial index and hosts no PDFs.

Rights-holders can request removal of any indexed material: we act within 24 hours, and removals are permanent (the nightly catalogue builder excludes them going forward). Full policy: TAKEDOWN.md.
